Privacy Policy & Artisan Data Sovereignty

At KalaSetu / KarigarSetu, digital privacy is rooted in artisan dignity. We strictly govern information handling under India's DPDP Act 2023, Ministry of Textiles guidelines, and decentralized ONDC registry protocols.

KarigarSetu does not participate in programmatic ad auctions, behavioral profiling, or private data brokerage. Every byte gathered fulfills direct operational touchpoints:

hub

Decentralized ONDC Protocol Orchestration

Transmitting encrypted catalog availability and delivery handshake tokens across ONDC Buyer Apps via the open-source Beckn protocol without exposing private buyer phone numbers or artisan accounts.

currency_rupee

Automated Fair-Pricing & Direct Remittances

Applying open benchmark formulas so that over 80% of consumer proceeds route directly to weaver accounts via NPCI switches, bypassing compounding middleman markups.

local_shipping

Cluster-to-Doorstep Logistics Coordination

Integrating directly with India Post's Dak Ghar Niryat Kendra (DNK) and verified regional rural parcel hubs to coordinate collection directly from rural master weaver clusters.

Many revered master artisans share loom histories and thread specifications through oral traditions in vernacular languages (Hindi, Bundelkhandi, Chhattisgarhi, Odia, Gujarati, Tamil). Our assistive voice portal is shielded by strict cryptographic guarantees:

task_alt Single Purpose Mandate

Audio input is processed strictly to extract craft descriptors, warp/weft specifications, and natural dye descriptions.

timer 48-Hour Hard Purge

Raw audio recordings are permanently overwritten from temporary memory caches within 48 hours of textual transcription.

block Zero Voice Cloning

Audio files are NEVER syndicated, leased, or utilized to train commercial generative voice, voice-clone, or synthetic speech models.

Data sharing strictly transpires via sovereign digital infrastructure protocols and accredited logistical partners. We do not sell data to advertisers or commercial brokers under any circumstance.
