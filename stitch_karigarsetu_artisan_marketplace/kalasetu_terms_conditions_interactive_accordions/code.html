Terms of Governance & Artisan Covenant

A transparent legal framework safeguarding rural handloom cooperatives, Pehchan-authenticated master craftspeople, and conscious patrons under India's Open Network for Digital Commerce (ONDC).

history_edu Last Updated: October 2024 • Version 2.5
search

KalaSetu (कला सेतु), operating under the registered network entity KarigarSetu Federation, operates strictly as an open digital commerce enabler conforming to India's Open Network for Digital Commerce (ONDC) protocols. The platform bridges verified rural weavers, self-help groups (SHGs), master artisan clusters (karigars), and conscious buyers.

hub Decentralized Tri-Party Operational Boundary

  • Direct Craft Contract: Sales transactions are executed directly between the purchaser and the designated artisan cooperative or master craftsman. KalaSetu facilitates verified cataloging and automated escrow settlement.
  • Logistics Execution: Physical parcel transit is managed via integrated ONDC logistics partners (including India Post Dak Ghar Niryat Kendras and accredited 3PL couriers). Tracking manifests are synchronized in real time.

By accessing or ordering through any connected network node, patrons agree that KarigarSetu's role is that of a trusted technology layer, digital authenticator, and fair wage escrow steward.

Every craftsman onboarding to the platform must possess a validated Pehchan Artisan Identity Card issued under the Development Commissioner (Handicrafts / Handlooms), Ministry of Textiles, Government of India.

verified_user Zero Mill Replica Policy & Account Forfeiture

Listing industrial Jacquard powerloom fabric as handloom (e.g., Chanderi, Jamdani), plastic/resin molds as Dokra bronze, or machine roller prints as Bagru hand-block will result in:

  • Permanent expulsion of the seller registry across the ONDC network node.
  • Freezing of pending escrows for reallocation to the Artisan Cluster Welfare Fund.
  • Statutory reporting to the Geographical Indications Registry under the GI of Goods Act, 1999.

Authentic listings carry verified GI identification tags and can be audited at any moment using our public decentralized registry hash.

bolt Ready-to-Ship Crafts

Sealed inventory present in cluster hubs. Dispatched within 48 hours of payment settlement. Cancellations are permitted until postal handover.

handyman Made-to-Order Looms

Crafted individually upon order confirmation (production cycle: 14 to 45 working days). Loom setup starts within 72 hours, after which cancellations cannot be honored.

Lead times depend on weather conditions for natural dyeing, mud curing, and metal firing. Patrons receive unedited milestone photographs via WhatsApp and SMS during loom progression.

Handmade artifacts do not possess industrial, robotic symmetry. Patrons acknowledge that minor nuances are hallmarks of authentic heritage handwork:

info Inherent Craft Traits (Non-Returnable)
  • Uneven slubs, selvedge yarn knots, and yarn tension changes on handlooms.
  • Organic vegetable dye shade variances influenced by sun drying or water mineral levels.
  • Clay pit smoke markings or Dokra lost-wax core porosity.
verified Permitted Return Conditions (7 Days)
  • Structural fracture or physical transit breakage during courier movement.
  • Shipping of entirely incorrect cluster craft design or dimension discrepancy (>8%).
  • Missing parts or intact tamper seal violations upon arrival.

No "change-of-mind" or remorse returns are entertained on custom or made-to-order weaves once warped on loom.

To prevent subjective contention and protect rural creators from unfair penalties, each artisan or cluster center supervisor conducts a mandatory pre-dispatch visual recording before affixing the security barcode seal.

videocam Automated Visual Evidence Protocol

In cases of reported transit breakage:

  1. Patrons submit unboxing photographs alongside the carrier consignment slip within 7 calendar days.
  2. The system cross-references the artisan’s pre-dispatch sealed capture.
  3. Upon confirmation of courier mishandling, full refund or remaking is authorized from the transit insurance reserve with zero financial forfeiture levied against the artisan.

Unresolved escalations route to the KalaSetu Artisan Ombudsman (ombudsman@karigarsetu.org) with a mandatory 7-day resolution window.

Unlike speculative commercial e-commerce markups of 300%–500%, KalaSetu maintains an open ledger model. For every ₹10,000 received:

Artisan Direct Fair Share (Raw Silk, Weaving Wages & Loom Amortization) 88.5% (₹8,850)
Secured Transit, Protective Boxing & India Post Pickup 7.0% (₹700)
ONDC Open Network Registry Node & Bank Escrow Gateway Processing 4.5% (₹450)

Settlement Timeline: Funds are automatically credited directly into the weaver SHG or individual artisan bank/UPI account within 48 banking hours of verified client delivery receipt.
