## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a balance between timeless cultural heritage and modern e-commerce utility. Built for an authentic Indian artisanal marketplace, the experience connects rural craft clusters directly with global patrons. 

The aesthetic is **Tactile Editorial Heritage**:
- **Sensory & Material**: Interfaces mimic raw silk, handloom khadi weaves, sun-baked clay, and vegetable-dyed textiles rather than stark tech surfaces.
- **Dignified & Transparent**: The design honors master artisans (*karigars*) not merely as vendors, but as national living treasures. Provenance, Geographical Indication (GI) verification, and regional cluster tracking take visual priority over aggressive sales banners.
- **Bilingual Nuance**: Dual-language typography pairing (English and Devanagari Hindi) functions seamlessly side by side across navigation, stamps, and metadata.
- **Contemporary Restraint**: Avoids ornamental clutter or stereotypical kitsch. Visual interest stems from precision editorial framing, generous warm whitespace, and refined border craft inspired by structural handloom selvedges.

## Colors

The color palette reflects natural, earth-derived dyes and traditional Indian raw materials:

- **Primary (Terracotta / Mitti)**: `#C85A32` (Deepened to `#9E3F1F` for pressed/hover states). Embodies terracotta pottery, brick kilns, and warm fired earth. Serves as the primary brand touchpoint, prominent action button fills, and key highlights.
- **Secondary (Neel Indigo)**: `#1E3A8A` (Deep Indigo `#152A65` for dark accents). Symbolizes royal heritage, Ajrakh printing, and natural indigo vats. Anchors trust, primary titles, artisan credentials, and high-order cards.
- **Tertiary (Haldi & Pital Brass)**: `#D97706` (Deep Brass `#B45309`). Evokes raw turmeric, marigolds, and brass metalcraft. Used sparingly for ratings, special edition indicators, process badges, and alert banners.
- **Neutral Canvas (Kora Parchment & Handloom Linen)**: Background surfaces sit at `#FAF7F2` (base canvas) with container surfaces elevated via `#F3ECE1` (card wash) and `#EAE0D0` (subtle dividers).
- **Ink & Typography (Kohl Charcoal)**: `#1C1A17` for crisp high-contrast body and titles, transitioning to `#3D3A36` for secondary descriptions, ensuring total readability without synthetic pitch black.
- **Provenance & Verification (Neem Leaf & GI Mint)**: Positive verification, Geographical Indication (GI) tags, and artisan authentication utilize `#15803D` text on `#DCFCE7` wash.

## Typography

The type system creates an editorial balance between classical literature and modern transactional clarity:

- **Headlines (`Playfair Display`)**: Employs an organic, transitional serif featuring dramatic stroke contrast and handcrafted cadence. Headlines carry the literary dignity of craft heritage, regional journals, and artisan manifestos.
- **Body & Controls (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Delivers clear modern readability across catalog items, craft origin descriptions, checkout forms, and numeric pricing tables.
- **Dual-Language & Devanagari Harmony**: When Devanagari script is displayed in the UI (e.g., regional craft titles like *चंदेरी सिल्क* or *मधुबनी*), set font weights equivalent to 500/600 to preserve optical density against English type.
- **Numerical & Pricing Formatting**: All monetary figures (`₹ INR`) utilize tabular lining figures within `Plus Jakarta Sans` for clean column alignment across invoices and pricing grids.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a structured 12-column grid system reminiscent of classical broadsheet publishing and artisanal museum folios:

- **Grid Framework**:
  - **Desktop (>= 1200px)**: 12-column fluid grid, max-width `1440px`, centered with `margin: 3rem` (48px) and `gutter: 1.5rem` (24px).
  - **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8-column grid with `margin: 2rem` (32px) and `gutter: 1.25rem` (20px).
  - **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column fluid layout with `margin-mobile: 1.25rem` (20px) and `gutter-mobile: 1rem` (16px).
- **Rhythm & Cadence**:
  - Use `space-md` (16px) for interior card padding, input fields, and compact meta rows.
  - Apply `space-lg` (24px) for card separation, form row gaps, and artisan biography sections.
  - Apply `space-xl` (40px) to establish breathable separation between major narrative blocks (e.g., craft process timelines, regional maps, and curated artisan rosters).

## Elevation & Depth

This design system rejects synthetic grey drop shadows in favor of **Warm Tactile Tonal Layers** and **Spiced Ambient Shadows**:

- **Ground Level (Canvas)**: `#FAF7F2` (Unbleached Kora Paper). Provides the raw organic foundation.
- **Layer 1 (Cards & Modules)**: `#F3ECE1`. Elevated purely through warm tone shift, bordered with a delicate `1px solid #E5D9C8`.
- **Layer 2 (Floating Cards & Hover Elevation)**:
  - Ambient shadow formula: `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(78, 42, 28, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(78, 42, 28, 0.04)`. 
  - Shadows are tinted with raw umber and terracotta tones rather than cold neutral black, evoking sunlit clay courtyards.
- **Layer 3 (Modals, Slide-overs, & Artisan Dossiers)**:
  - Deep warm shadow: `box-shadow: 0 20px 48px -8px rgba(30, 26, 23, 0.16), 0 6px 16px -2px rgba(30, 26, 23, 0.08)`.
  - Combined with a backdrop veil of `rgba(28, 26, 23, 0.45)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`.
- **Selvedge Accent Edges**: Premium modules and provenance certificates utilize a 1px dashed or dual-stitch border rule (`#D1BEA8`) referencing handloom borders (*kinari*).

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **Soft, Handcrafted Architectural Geometry** (`roundedness: 1`):

- **Default Radii (0.25rem / 4px)**: Applied to input fields, buttons, and micro-tags. Evokes crisp block-printed edges, clean loom heddles, and chiseled stone craft.
- **Medium Radii (`rounded-lg` / 0.5rem / 8px)**: Applied to product listing cards, artisan portrait frames, and cluster preview modules.
- **Expanded Radii (`rounded-xl` / 0.75rem / 12px)**: Reserved exclusively for regional provenance banners and modal sheets.
- **Notched & Stamp Shapes**: GI Tags and Artisan Seals may utilize an optional 8-sided chamfer or scalloped postage-stamp border mask to emphasize authentic government-certified origins.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Solid Terracotta background (`#C85A32`), pure white text, 4px border radius. On hover: shifts to Deep Terracotta (`#9E3F1F`) with subtle ambient warmth. Padding: `12px 24px` for `label-lg`.
- **Secondary Action (Artisan / Heritage)**: Deep Indigo background (`#1E3A8A`), white text. Used for "Meet the Karigar", "Explore Cluster", and "Custom Commission". Hover: `#152A65`.
- **Tertiary / Outline**: Transparent surface with `1.5px solid #C85A32`, text in `#C85A32`. Hover: background fills with `#C85A32` at 8% opacity.
- **Text Link**: Charcoal `#1C1A17` with a terracotta underline offset by 4px.

### GI Verification Badges & Provenance Tags
- **GI Tag Certified**: Mint green pill or soft rectangular chip (`#DCFCE7` background, `1px solid #86EFAC`, `#15803D` text). Preceded by a woven seal icon.
- **Craft Cluster Tag**: Terracotta tint (`#FBEBE5` background, `#C85A32` text, `1px solid #F5C6B7`).
- **Dual-Language Micro-Labels**: Secondary small caption pairing native Hindi craft nomenclature with English (e.g., `चंदेरी • Chanderi, MP` in `label-sm`).

### Artisan Provenance Cards
- Surface: `#F3ECE1` with `1px solid #E5D9C8`.
- Structure: Includes an organic circular/soft-square portrait of the master artisan with their generation lineage (e.g., *"5th Generation Weaver"*), cluster location pin, and authentic artisan signature mark.
- Micro-storytelling: Features an italic quote in `Playfair Display` describing the technique.

### Cards (Product & Craft Showcase)
- Flat base with an image-dominant frame (ratio 4:5 for textiles, 1:1 for brassware and pottery).
- Soft `#F3ECE1` parchment background under text compartments.
- Subtle inner border to simulate hand-framed archival art paper.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background: `#FAF7F2` canvas with a solid `1px solid #D4C5B3` border.
- Active / Focus State: Transition to `1.5px solid #C85A32` outline with zero ring spread.
- Label: Deep charcoal ink `#3D3A36` in `label-md`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Custom box with 3px radius for checkboxes, full circle for radios.
- Unchecked: `1.5px solid #A89682` border over `#FAF7F2`.
- Checked: Fill `#C85A32` with a clean white embroidered-style checkmark icon.

### Weave Dividers
- Section dividers alternate between standard hairline rules (`1px solid #E5D9C8`) and a repeating two-tone dashed geometric border representing warp and weft yarn structures.
